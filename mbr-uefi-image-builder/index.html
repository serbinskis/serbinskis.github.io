Image Preparation Guide

MBR/EXE mode only has ~19.5 KB of space after compression and supports at most 255 colors. Keep input files under 100 KB (ideally ~20 KB), avoid anti-aliasing/gradients, and do not place too many multi-colored pixels in one area.
